## Required evidence distinction
ETF OHLC directional labels do not determine option execution price, IV evolution, decay, volatility exposure, chain liquidity or obtainable fills. No 0DTE edge/return claim follows from EMA/OR signals or cost-adjusted ETF proxies. Paper engines can have optimistic or undocumented fill behavior; their behavior must itself be characterized before interpretation. None of that behavior is tested here.

A future read-only scanner could stop at research alerts with unavailable/freshness/conflict displays; paper execution is optional and separately gated. Even after a hypothetical paper trial, live capital/risk/security/legal/broker feasibility would need NEW human authorization and independent controls review. This checklist grants none. Do not connect providers or run simulated orders to fill checklist evidence under H-005; mark missing evidence UNKNOWN and request exact scope.
